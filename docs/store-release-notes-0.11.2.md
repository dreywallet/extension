# Drey extension 0.11.2 Store notes

## What's new

Drey now keeps routine balance refreshes focused on the account you are
viewing. Wallets with many accounts remain responsive without doing hidden
account work in the background.

## Reviewer focus

- Switching accounts refreshes the newly selected account and preserves the
  last verified balance for accounts that are not open.
- The Home screen shows compact syncing feedback while the selected account is
  updating.
- Restore, manual rescan, Extended scan, and conflict recovery continue to
  check the broader wallet when explicitly needed.
- No recovery words, passwords, private keys, funded wallet, or reviewer
  credential is required to review these changes.
