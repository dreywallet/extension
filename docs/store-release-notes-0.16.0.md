# Drey 0.16.0 Store notes

## What's new

Drey beta 0.16.0 makes sending bitcoin simpler and clearer. The Send screen
shows how much you can spend with a one-tap Max, keeps what you typed if the
popup closes, and points to problems right where they are. Fee choices show
roughly what they cost and how long they take, and the review now leads with
how much you are sending, to whom, and the total.

Drey also opens its welcome screen after install, takes "Back up now"
straight to your recovery phrase, explains what to do if you forget your
password, and uses one consistent Back control and button style throughout.

This update adds no browser permissions.
